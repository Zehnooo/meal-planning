Meal System

Built around weight gain, steady energy, low cleanup, Aldi-first shopping, and meals that feel like takeout at home.

Default rule: if a meal needs chopping, thawing, or more than two pans on a weekday, simplify it before cooking.

Targets

  • Gain from 125 toward 150–160 lb
  • Aim around 150g protein/day
  • Use calorie-dense add-ons: cheese, queso, milk, rice, potatoes, granola
  • Breakfast counts even when tiny

Cookware Map

  • Induction burner: beef, eggs, pasta, steak
  • Ninja XL: fries, orange chicken, potatoes, toasted wraps, pizza-style foods
  • Microwave: weak but useful for rice, queso, leftovers
  • Blender: protein shakes when cooking is not happening
  • Slow cooker: optional fish fallback, not a daily dependency

Weekday Rules

  • Max two pots or pans
  • Use pre-cooked chicken unless you specifically have energy
  • No mushrooms, tuna, sushi, or onions by default
  • Leftovers are only expected for one or two days

Prep Staples

  • Ground beef: cook 1 lb, use for bowls, burgers, pasta, potatoes
  • Rice: make or buy enough for 2–3 bowls
  • Lettuce/spinach: bagged greens beat chopping
  • Sauces prevent boredom: queso, ranch, Caesar, burger sauce, taco sauce

Breakfast Ladder

  • 2 minutes: yogurt, granola, banana
  • 5 minutes: protein shake with milk, banana, oats
  • 10 minutes: sausage egg cheese muffin
  • No appetite: milk or shake still moves the day forward

Failure Plan

  • Too tired: shake, cereal, frozen meal, or orange chicken bowl
  • Forgot prep: microwave rice or tortillas
  • Bored: swap sauce before swapping the whole meal
  • Cleanup dread: Ninja XL plus one bowl
